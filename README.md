# Kalimantan Wildfire: Spatial and Economic Impact Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard project exploring the multidimensional impacts of forest fires in Kalimantan, Indonesia, covering public health, air pollution, economic losses, and transboundary haze.

## Project Overview

This project uses data visualization and analytical storytelling to examine wildfire-related impacts and present insights through three dashboard sections:

- **Operations:** Fire hotspot and weather monitoring.
- **Impact:** Health impacts, PM2.5 concentration, vulnerable populations, and regional diplomatic responses.
- **Executive:** Economic impact scenarios, mitigation strategies, and wildfire causality analysis.

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for health and environmental indicators.
- Provincial comparisons of respiratory illness and emergency care.
- Scatter plot exploring PM2.5 concentration and affected vulnerable populations.
- Donut chart showing the geographical reach of transboundary haze.
- Diplomatic response monitoring across affected regions.
- Economic impact and mitigation scenario analysis.

## Data Modeling

The Power BI data model includes several datasets connected through dimension tables:

- **Dim_Tanggal:** Date dimension for temporal analysis.
- **Dim_Provinsi:** Province dimension for regional analysis.
- **Fact and supporting tables:** Fire hotspots, weather conditions, health impacts, wildfire causes, economic damage scenarios, mitigation costs, fire actor breakdowns, and transboundary haze impacts.

Relationships are designed to connect relevant datasets and support cross-table analysis in Power BI.
